Telegram — when and why will it message me?

🔴 New or worse warningSent when a monitored rule becomes Watch/Serious or changes severity. Check the result and source; it is not a sell command.
✅ Warning clearedSent when a previously flagged rule returns to Clear. It only clears that one rule—not all market risk.
📄 Company filing / your changesSEC filing alerts say “something new was filed”; checkbox and override messages confirm your own changes. Filings need reading.

Timing: Market/FRED checks run on scheduled weekdays; optional quotes are polled hourly on weekdays; SEC filings are checked in rotation; a daily digest can be sent on market-check days. Feed publication, Cron timing and Telegram delivery introduce delays. There are no guaranteed instant crash alerts.

What is automated, partly automated, or manual?

Monitoring coverage — automatic versus manual

All 22 checklist indicators mapped

Automated = numerical or filing-feed evaluation. Partial = selected data is automatically collected, but a complete judgment requires human review. Manual = no trustworthy complete free feed. A missing feed does NOT check a box as Clear.

Read the data limitations

Data quality & coverage

Nasdaq trend and drawdowns use the NASDAQ-100 index from FRED, not the QQQM ETF. FRED observations are usually daily closes and can be delayed. VIX and high-yield spreads are also delayed published observations. Finnhub quotes are optional and availability depends on your key. SEC alerts detect new filings, not their economic meaning.

Monthly EPS consensus revisions, holdings look-through, true AI-specific capex ROI, most breadth measures, supplier order quality, weekly RSI and any ticker without permitted history remain manual. Unassessed and stale readings are never presented as safe.

Automation is designed to reduce repetitive checking; it cannot guarantee detection before a fast crash.

Test your assumptions

Valuation scenarios

Use before buying a stock, after earnings, or if its valuation looks expensive. This calculator does not fetch current prices: you enter a consistent stock price and future EPS estimate.

When to useAfter fresh earnings guidance, price changes or before making a new stock investment.
What it calculatesThree implied future prices using future EPS × future P/E, plus upside/downside versus today.
How to use the answerInvestigate whether the future EPS and P/E assumptions are realistic. If plausible cases are poor, document that risk in your stock review and journal.

Earnings × P/E scenario analysis

Simple scenario prices = EPS × P/E; these are not discounted fair values. Keep EPS and P/E definitions consistent (forward vs trailing, GAAP vs adjusted). No dividends or timing modeled.

Historical lesson

In March 2000, the market-weighted P/E for a group of 18 large technology firms exceeded 125× according to Federal Reserve research. That is materially different from many profitable AI leaders today. But even a 25× forward P/E can be risky if the forward EPS estimate is too optimistic.

Understand the trend

Moving averages explained

Use during your weekly market review or after a selloff. Enter QQQM data from a daily chart. Live monitoring currently uses a Nasdaq-100 index proxy—not these manual QQQM values.

When to useDuring a weekly check or following a sharp price move.
What it calculatesHow far a manually entered QQQM closing price is above/below its 50- and 200-day simple moving averages.
How to use the answerUse as trend context alongside breadth, earnings and credit. Below 200D is not an automatic exit. The live Worker checks Nasdaq-100 index series separately.

QQQM daily moving-average monitor

Use 1D chart, 200-session SMA, not 200-week SMA. The conventional 200-day indicator is usually SMA, not EMA. Enter a dated observation; this tool does not fetch market data.

Distance above/below 200-day SMA
Distance above/below 50-day SMA

Distance = (daily close ÷ daily SMA − 1) × 100. Being far above a rising SMA signals an established uptrend but can also mean a late breakdown signal. Compare distance with QQQM's own history, not an arbitrary universal cutoff.
